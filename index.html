About Lee Enterprise

Survey, engineering & construction under one roof

M/s Lee Enterprise is a Naharlagun-based firm established in 2019 and led by a B.Tech civil engineer. We combine LiDAR drone mapping, DGPS surveying and practical design experience to deliver infrastructure and buildings that suit the hills, rivers and monsoon of the Northeast.

  • One team from field survey to drawings, estimates, DPR and construction — no hand-offs.
  • Own DJI Matrice 350 with Zenmuse L2 LiDAR — sees the ground under forest cover.
  • Supply of building materials, electrical goods, stationery and government orders.
  • Clients follow their project online — progress photos, updates and direct messages.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does M/s Lee Enterprise offer?

Drone and LiDAR survey, DGPS and topographic survey, road and infrastructure design, DPR preparation, architectural and structural design, water supply design, construction, and supply of building materials, electrical goods and stationery.

Where are you based and which areas do you serve?

Model Village, Naharlagun, Papum Pare District, Arunachal Pradesh – 791110. We work across Arunachal Pradesh and the Northeast, and on highway surveys beyond.

Can your drones survey forested or hilly land?

Yes. Our Zenmuse L2 LiDAR measures the ground through gaps in vegetation, giving reliable contours and sections in steep, forested terrain.

How do I follow my project's progress?

We give you a private project code. Open the Client Portal, enter the code and see updates, photos and drawings — and send us messages or files.

How long does a typical project take?

A small site survey can be done in a day or two; full design and DPR packages usually take a few weeks. We give a schedule with every quote.
